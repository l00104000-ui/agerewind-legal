Privacy Policy

App: AgeRewind  ·  Effective date: 2026-07-02
Provider: KAI LI  ·  Contact: bodypilotlab@proton.me  ·  Governing law: China

This is a plain-language privacy policy reflecting the actual build: on-device deterministic HealthSpan scoring, and a third-party AI provider (DeepSeek) used only for written plans/explanations. AgeRewind is a non-medical wellness app.

Summary

We collect the minimum needed to run a personalized, adaptive fitness experience: your account, your questionnaire answers, your workouts/check-ins, your self-entered strength-test results and goals, and aggregated health metrics derived on your device. Your HealthSpan Score and HealthSpan Age are computed on your device by a deterministic engine; we store only the resulting summary values, not your raw Apple Health records. We use a third-party AI provider for written explanations and to turn your goal into a plan. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account — when you sign in with Apple: an Apple user identifier, and your email/name only if you choose to share them. We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Questionnaire / profile — answers you provide (e.g., age, sex, goals, experience, availability, self-reported constraints, and optional body measurements such as height/weight/waist).
  • Derived health metrics — features your device computes from Apple Health, such as 30-day average resting heart rate, VO₂max, weekly active minutes, and body composition. Raw HealthKit records (individual workouts, heart-rate samples, etc.) stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Strength-test results — counts/times you enter for the optional baseline test (push-ups, chair stand, single-leg balance).
  • Goals — the focus areas, target, and timeframe you choose.
  • Scores over time — your computed HealthSpan Score and HealthSpan Age history (derived summary values), used to show trends.
  • Training data — generated plans and your check-ins/logs.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription (via Apple/RevenueCat). We do not receive your payment card details; purchases are processed by Apple.
  • Basic technical data — we use Apple's standard crash diagnostics to fix bugs. We do not embed third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • Compute your HealthSpan Score and HealthSpan Age on your device (deterministic, not AI).
  • Generate and adapt your training plans, and (for subscribers) written explanations of your results.
  • Show your progress and trends.
  • Manage your account and subscription.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse (e.g., per-account free-usage limits).

We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell it.

AI processing

Your scores and HealthSpan Age are computed on your device and are not produced by AI. We use a third-party AI provider only to (a) turn your chosen goal and focus areas into a training plan, and (b) for subscribers, write a plain-language explanation of your already-computed results. For these, our backend sends the relevant inputs — your questionnaire answers, your goal/focus, and derived summary values (e.g., your score, HealthSpan Age, and pillar percentiles) — to DeepSeek (an AI provider) on our servers. We do not send raw Apple Health records. Outputs are validated for safety before you see them. DeepSeek processes these inputs under its own terms and privacy policy; we send only the minimum needed to produce your plan/explanation and never the raw Apple Health records.

Service providers (processors)

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, HealthKit (on-device).
  • Supabase — authentication, database, and serverless functions hosting (region: Tokyo, ap-northeast-1).
  • DeepSeek — AI generation of training plans and written explanations. DeepSeek is operated from China; we send only derived summary values (never raw Apple Health records).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.

Each processes data on our behalf under their standard terms / data-processing agreements.

Storage, security, retention

  • Data is stored in our Supabase project (Tokyo); each user's rows are isolated via Row-Level Security.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider.
  • We retain your data while your account is active, and delete it within 30 days of account deletion (backups may persist for a short additional period before rotation).

Your rights

  • Access / export / correct / delete your data: email us at bodypilotlab@proton.me and we will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account and associated data in the app's account settings, or by emailing us.
  • Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have additional rights (access, portability, erasure, objection). Contact us to exercise them.

Children

This app is rated 17+ and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

International transfers

Data is processed and stored in Japan (Supabase, Tokyo) and processed by DeepSeek in China. If you use the app from the EEA/UK, these are international transfers; we rely on the providers' contractual safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will post the new effective date here and, for material changes, notify you in-app.
